AI Safety & Privacy

Our Child-First Pledge
Last Updated: August 2026 · Version 2.4
At LULO, we believe generative AI should empower family bonding and language growth without compromising privacy or innocence. Here is how we safeguard your child and data.
🛡️
1. Zero Public AI Training on Child Voice
• No Foundation Training: Your child’s voice recordings, spoken phrases, and roleplay interactions are strictly isolated in a private secure enclave. They are never used to train public generative models (like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or open-source speech models).
• Zero Data Brokering: We will never sell, license, or monetize your family’s voice or behavioral data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
👶
2. Age-Appropriate AI Guardrails
• Pedagogical Safety Filters: Every storyline, prompt, and AI response is vetted by multi-tiered pedagogical classifiers to ensure positive reinforcement, constructive emotional tone, and cultural warmth.
• Real-time Harm Prevention: Frightening themes, mature topics, violence, and sensitive concepts are actively intercepted and redirected to gentle, encouraging topics before reaching your child’s device.
🔒
3. COPPA & GDPR-K Certified Protection
• Strict Global Standards: LULO complies with the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), European GDPR-K, and Canadian PIPEDA standards.
• End-to-End Encryption: All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3, and data stored at rest uses military-grade AES-256 encryption.
• Zero Ad SDKs: No advertising trackers, behavioral analytics, or third-party tracking scripts are embedded in our mobile software.
⚙️
4. 100% Parental Control & Erasure
• Per-Session Recording Control: You can toggle "Save Transcript to History" off before starting any adventure. When toggled off, no audio or conversation log is stored.
• 1-Click Instant Deletion: Parents hold absolute ownership. You may export or permanently delete your family’s entire conversation history, vocabulary insights, and account data at any time from Settings.
🧸
5. Physical Toy Hardware Security
• Secure Local Pairing: The companion toy connects exclusively to your authenticated parent smartphone via secure Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).
• No Open Inbound Ports: The toy contains no camera, no location GPS tracking, and no direct open internet connection.
